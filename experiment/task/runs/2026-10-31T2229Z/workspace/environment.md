# Where the analyst will work

- The analyst works alone, in one folder. The folder holds two files: `trips.csv`, the week of trips, and `dataset.md`, the description of its columns.
- Software: Python 3.13 with pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn, scipy, statsmodels, scikit-learn and Jupyter. The analyst has internet access and may install other packages.
- The deliverable is one Jupyter notebook named `analysis.ipynb`, saved in that folder.
- No one is available to answer questions while the work is under way. Where something is unclear, the analyst makes a reasonable assumption and states it in the notebook.
